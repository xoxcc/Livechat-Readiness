D · What happens after a WhatsApp option

The call ends and 3CX calls our API. Two separate checks follow: did 3CX reach our API, and did the WhatsApp template reach the customer. The two boxes in bold green are where you come in.

API layer failure

3CX could not reach our API. This is a connection issue between 3CX and us. Owner: 3CX. It retries with the same call ID, then logs and reports to GPTBots and XOX. Agents do nothing in LiveDesk because no conversation exists. If customers complain they pressed 7 and got nothing, tell your TL with the time and phone number.

WhatsApp delivery failure

The template did not reach the customer's phone. Owner: us / LiveDesk. The conversation is tagged template not delivered. Agents work these from the tagged view: try the follow-up template once, then a voice callback if your TL approves, and note the outcome before closing.

StepWhat to doWhy it matters

08

Verification & PDPA

Chat leaves a written record on the customer's phone and ours. Treat everything you type as permanent.

How to verify on chat

  • Confirm the chat is from the registered number, or ask for the XOX number concerned.
  • Ask for the full name as registered.
  • Ask for the last 4 digits of IC or passport, never the full number.
  • If it does not match, do not disclose anything; offer the general information only.

Never ask for or send

  • Full IC or passport number, or a photo of the IC
  • OTP, PIN, PUK, or account passwords
  • Card numbers, CVV or online banking details
  • Another customer's details, even to a family member, without authorisation

If the customer sends sensitive data anyway

Do not repeat it back. Reply: "For your safety, please don't share full IC or banking details here. I only need the last 4 digits." Then inform your TL so the attachment can be handled under the data protection procedure.

How targets are tracked

  • FRT, AHT and concurrency come from the livechat platform's own reporting.
  • CSAT is captured through the post-chat rating prompt.
  • CM QA Score is scored in the CM tool, using the same rubric as calls, adapted for chat transcripts.
  • Reviewed weekly during hypercare (Phase 4), then monthly once stable.

Blended scorecard (call + chat)

Agents handle both channels, so their monthly scorecard blends call and chat. Each KPI is first scored against its own channel's target, then the two channel scores are weighted by how many interactions the agent handled on each channel. Monthly checks are not blended.

FAQ

Does the AI answer the Business WhatsApp (011-1333 7010)?

No. That line is dealer-only (SIM replacement and postpaid termination) and stays fully human, handled by Nadiah and Team Leaders on Engagelab. The AI chatbot only answers chats coming from 03-8800 5500 and 03-7884 2323.

Will I still take calls?

Yes. Voice continues. Your TL assigns chat, call or both per shift through the schedule.

What if the customer only wants to talk on the phone?

Offer a voice callback, note the number and preferred time, and pass it to your TL. Never force a customer to stay on chat.

The AI gave the customer wrong information. What do I do?

Correct it politely without blaming the system, then report the chat ID to QA so the AI content can be fixed.

Can I use my own wording instead of canned replies?

Yes, as long as the standards in section 06 are met. Canned replies are a starting point, not a script.

I haven't done the typing test yet.

Complete it on typingtest.com and submit your result through the digital form before your training session.

Something in this pack is wrong or missing.

Tell your TL or QA. This page is updated during rollout and changes appear for everyone the next time they open it.

I can't sign in.

Use the same email and password as the CM Tool. If you've forgotten it, reset it in the CM Tool or ask your Team Lead. If you're new and don't have an account yet, your TL can record your attendance for you.
